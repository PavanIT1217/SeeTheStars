Other Worlds

Planets around other stars

Name: ______________________Date: ____________

Key facts

PSR B1257+12 planets
The first planets ever confirmed outside our Solar System were found in a very strange place: orbiting a pulsar, the spinning leftover core of an exploded star. Nobody expected planets there!
51 Pegasi b
51 Pegasi b was the first planet found orbiting a star like our Sun. It shocked astronomers: a giant planet like Jupiter, but so close to its star that it races around it in just over four days.
HD 209458 b (Osiris)
HD 209458 b was the first exoplanet caught passing in front of its star, and the first whose atmosphere was detected. The star’s heat is so intense that the planet’s gas is boiling away into space, forming a comet-like tail.
TRAPPIST-1
TRAPPIST-1 is a small, cool star with seven rocky planets, all roughly the size of Earth, packed closer to their star than Mercury is to the Sun. Three of them are in the zone where liquid water might be possible.
Kepler-186f
Kepler-186f was the first Earth-sized planet discovered in the habitable zone of another star. It gets about a third of the energy Earth gets from the Sun, so if it has water, it may be cold – but not necessarily frozen.
K2-18 b
K2-18 b is a planet bigger than Earth but smaller than Neptune, in its star’s habitable zone. The James Webb Space Telescope found methane and carbon dioxide in its atmosphere, and some scientists think it could be covered by a deep ocean.
