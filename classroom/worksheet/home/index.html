Key facts

Earth
Earth is the third planet from the Sun and the only place in the universe where we know life exists. About 71% of its surface is covered by liquid water, and a thin blanket of air protects us from the cold and radiation of space.
International Space Station
The International Space Station is the biggest thing humans have ever built in space – a laboratory and home where astronauts from many countries live and work. People have been living on board without a break since November 2000.
The Moon
The Moon is Earth’s only natural satellite. Scientists think it formed about 4.5 billion years ago when a Mars-sized world crashed into the young Earth and the debris clumped together. Its gravity creates the ocean tides.
The Sun
The Sun is a star – a giant ball of hot gas. In its core, hydrogen atoms smash together to make helium, releasing the energy that lights and warms our planet. It holds about 99.8% of all the mass in the Solar System.
