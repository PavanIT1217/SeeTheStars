See the Stars in the classroom

A free, guided astronomy course for primary and middle school: 12 chapters from the Earth and Moon to galaxies and the Big Bang, each with a quiz, plus a Sky Finder that turns every clear night into homework kids actually want to do.

How it works

  1. Sign in as a grown-up on My journey and create a class here.
  2. Share the 6-letter code. Students enter it with a nickname on their own device – no email or account needed.
  3. See who explored, spotted and finished what. Only nicknames and counts are shared, nothing else.

One per chapter: key facts, the chapter quiz and a “find it in the sky” checklist.
