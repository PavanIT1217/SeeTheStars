Other Worlds

Planets around other stars

For thousands of years people wondered whether other stars have planets too. The first ones were found only about 30 years ago. Today more than 6,000 are confirmed: scorching giants, lava worlds and maybe ocean worlds. Somewhere among them there could be a planet like Earth.
