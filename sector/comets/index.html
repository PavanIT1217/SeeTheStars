Comets & Space Visitors

Icy wanderers and interstellar travellers

Comets are “dirty snowballs” left over from the birth of the Solar System. When one swings close to the Sun, its ice turns to gas and it grows a glowing head and tails millions of kilometres long. And recently, astronomers have spotted visitors from other star systems racing through our neighbourhood!
